§ Service 04 — Systems

Business systems and workflow design for small teams

Your tools, data and daily routines designed as one system — shaped around how your people actually work.

Book a free 30-minute call

Who it’s for

  • Businesses running on spreadsheets, sticky notes and memory.
  • Teams using five tools that each hold part of the picture.
  • Owners who want to step back without things falling apart.

Problems it fixes

  • No single place to see what’s booked, sold, owed or overdue.
  • The same information typed into several systems.
  • New staff taking weeks to learn “how we do things here.”
  • Reports that take hours to pull together by hand.

How it works

What happens, step by step.

  1. A.

    Understand

    We map the jobs your business does every day and the information each one needs.

  2. B.

    Design

    I design a simple setup: which tool owns what, and how information moves between them.

  3. C.

    Build

    We set it up, connect it, and automate the repetitive steps.

  4. D.

    Document

    A short guide your team can follow, so the system outlasts any one person.

Systems: questions

Do we need to replace our current software?

Usually not. Most of the gain comes from connecting and simplifying what you already have.

Can you automate repetitive tasks?

Yes. Copying data between tools, reminders, follow-ups and routine reports are the most common things automated.

Will it be too complicated for my team?

No — if it’s too complicated for your team, it isn’t finished. Simple to use is part of the brief.
